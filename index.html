Where your £5 goes

The Mae Murray Foundation supports over 2,500 member families across Northern Ireland facing exclusion through disability, age or medical condition. Our Resource Library lends wheelchairs, powered mobility, buggies, seating and sensory equipment free at the point of access, and our inclusive beaches, fun days, Teen Hub, Stay and Play and family holidays bring people together for all ages and abilities.

We're a lived experience organisation. Our members design what we deliver, and every raffle ticket helps keep our Resource Library and events free for the families who need them.
